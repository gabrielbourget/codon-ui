Before each family, read its complete canonical source, inherited types/behavior and suites, then instantiate the exact capability/scenario rows and expected outcomes. Preserve controlled values, existing pins/palette, package ownership, sealed evidence, normal hooks and local Git identity. Use the established shared TypeScript/native style/state/ref/overlay patterns. Consolidate novel API/architecture/dependency/theme/registry/tooling decisions for owner review and continue unaffected rows while those decisions are pending. Branch pushes and library PR creation are authorized under §9.1. Merge, publication, workflow/distribution modifications, sibling edits and iPhone signing remain unauthorized.

The finish line is every applicable acceptance cell in sections [3.1.1–3.1.9](#31-acceptance-rubric), including the installed Android/iOS matrices. Stable `CAT.<manifest-id>.G01`–`G09` keys identify evidence cells only. The [evidence matrix](experiments.md#3-catalogue-acceptance-matrix) records the individual cells and proof links. `OPEN` means not yet reconciled or executed, not a failed test; `FAIL` and `BLOCKED` remain unresolved. A source/mock pass cannot close an installed cell. An agent-authored exclusion, a merely passing repeat or a diagnostic receipt cannot close a required cell. `N/A` needs the canonical-contract reason; `OWNER-DEFERRED` needs the exact existing/new owner authority and remains visibly deferred rather than passing. Existing physical/ARM/owner-visual deferrals are retained, not widened to emulator/simulator acceptance.

RN-TEXT-01 and original ComboBox failures keep their identities. They stop dependent installed/query propagation, not independent catalogue construction. Do not conduct further XCTest/LLDB/startup investigations while independent mandatory construction work remains. A later lab repair requires a materially new hypothesis and one goal-wide bounded attempt; repeated unchanged timeout investigations do not restart the workstream. If a required cell is externally blocked, preserve it, move to another incomplete cell and keep the goal incomplete. Exhausting all independently actionable work produces an explicit blocking decision, not a claim of completion.
